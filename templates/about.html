About SilentGuardian

SilentGuardian is an AI-based elderly safety monitoring system designed to notice situations where an elder may silently need help.

What is SilentGuardian?

SilentGuardian uses a camera-based monitoring system to observe posture and movement. When something appears concerning, the system checks whether the person is okay before raising an alert.

How Does It Work?

The system analyzes movement and posture, observes suspicious situations, and performs a voice wellness check. If there is no safe response, an alert can be triggered.

Privacy First

SilentGuardian is designed with privacy in mind. The concept focuses on detecting situations that may require attention without requiring wearable devices.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple: help families feel more reassured while allowing elderly people to remain independent and comfortable.
